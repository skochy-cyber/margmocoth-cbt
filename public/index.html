Features

Everything for a smooth exam day

The portal handles sign-in, exam access, timing, monitoring and results in one place.

Secure sign-in

Only students registered by the college can log in with their name, level, department and password.

Access-code exams

Your lecturer gives you a code on exam day. Without it, the exam stays locked.

Live countdown

See your time left while you answer. When time is up, your exam is submitted automatically.

Fair and monitored

The portal watches for rule breaking, such as leaving the exam page, so every student gets a fair chance.

Network-aware

If your internet drops and returns, it is logged as a network issue, not as cheating.

Results online

Once the college releases your results, view them from your dashboard anytime.

How it works

Four steps from login to result

You only need a phone or computer with internet and your login details.

  1. Sign in

    Enter your full name, level, department and password from the examination office.

  2. Open your exam

    On your dashboard, choose the exam available to you.

  3. Enter access code

    Type the code from your lecturer to unlock the exam and start the timer.

  4. Write and submit

    Answer before the timer ends, then submit. Results show when the college releases them.

Before you start

Get ready for exam day

A few simple habits keep your exam smooth and free of problems.

  • Charge your deviceUse a stable internet connection.
  • Stay on the exam pageLeaving it can be counted as a violation.
  • Keep your password privateNever share it with anyone.
  • Be earlySign in and have your access code ready before the exam starts.

Support

Having trouble signing in?

My name is not found

Type your name the way the college registered it, and choose the same level and department.

I forgot my password

Contact the examination office. Do not share your password with anyone.

The page will not load

Check your internet connection, then refresh the page.

I do not have an access code

Your lecturer gives the access code for each exam. Ask them before the exam starts.

My internet dropped during an exam

Reconnect and continue. The portal records it as a network issue. Tell your lecturer if the problem continues.
