Security Settings

Manage exam integrity and monitoring

Enable Webcam Monitoring Off
Capture periodic snapshots from student webcam during exam for proctoring. Also enables face capture before the exam begins.
Enforce Fullscreen Mode Off
Force students into fullscreen. Attempting to go back, close the tab, minimize the window, open the notification bar, or use split-screen will be recorded as a violation.
Enable Anti-Cheat Detection Off
Detect and record tab switches, copy/paste attempts, and blocked keyboard shortcuts.
Violation Volume AI Powered Balanced
Sets how sharp the AI monitoring is, from 1 to 100. At 1 the AI captures slowly and forgives short movements. At 100 it checks the camera many times a second and flags even small head turns, brief talking and short absences. It adjusts Strict Face Anti-Cheat, Restrict Side Look, Restrict Voice and how fast leaving the exam window is counted.
1 · Gentle50 · Balanced100 · Very sharp
Enable Strict Face Anti-Cheat AI Powered Off
Uses AI face detection on the webcam feed in real-time. Triggers a violation when more than 1 face is detected, or the student looks away for 5+ seconds. Requires webcam to be enabled. Also triggers face capture before the exam.
Allow Student Face Capture Off
When enabled, students can open the Face Capture page on their dashboard and take a reference selfie. Each student can only capture once — the page locks after their first upload. Disable this after all students have captured to prevent any further changes. Admins can reset individual students from the Student Profiles page.
Restrict Side Look AI Powered Off
When ON, students must maintain eye contact with the exam screen. The AI monitors the webcam — if a student turns their head sideways for more than 3 seconds, a violation is recorded. Requires webcam to be enabled. Also triggers face capture before the exam.
Restrict Voice (Mouth Movement Detection) AI Powered Off
When ON, the AI monitors lip/mouth movement via the webcam. If the student's mouth is detected moving (talking or whispering) for more than 3 seconds, a violation is recorded. Requires webcam to be enabled. Also triggers face capture before the exam.
