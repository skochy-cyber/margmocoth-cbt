Tips for a Good Photo
Follow these for best results
  • Make sure your face is well-lit — face a window or bright light source
  • Only your face should be in the frame — no other people visible
  • Keep a neutral expression and look straight at the camera
  • Remove sunglasses, hats, or any face coverings
  • Hold the phone at eye level — not from above or below
