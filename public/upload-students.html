CSV Format

Required CSV format:

name,matric,email,department,level
John Doe,MAT001,john@example.com,Medical Laboratory,100L
Jane Smith,MAT002,,Public Health,200L
Ali Hassan,MAT003,ali@example.com,HIM,300L
Matric and email are optional but let students sign in with them. The old name,department,level format still works.
Password is auto-assigned from the active generated password for each department. Department and class names must match exactly as created in Settings.
